    04

    What changes per business

    Three tiers. The first already sits in their TapCard design, the second is typed once at signup, the third never changes — which is what makes same-day possible.

    Pulled automatically

    From their existing design

    Brand colour, card ground, logo and business name already live in the pass design they signed off on. The insert reads the same record — no second approval round, and the stand always matches the wallet card.

    Typed once

    At activation

    Headline choice, their Google Place ID, and which tag ID you're binding. Thirty seconds on /biz/:id, standing at their counter.

    Never changes

    The plate

    Trim size, safe area, QR quiet zone, and the 30 mm disc target the tap ring registers against. Fixed across every client and body, which is why one imposition sheet serves the whole product line — and why the ring has to print dead-on over the tag.

    Changes without reprinting

    The destination

    Review page today, menu next month, loyalty signup for a promo week. The tag holds your URL and nothing else, so this is a dropdown — not a reprint, not a re-tag, not a visit.
