# cards/

Ten finished credit cards (85.6 × 54 mm), one on each of the landscape
compositions, every one wearing the contactless hand mark and the Google review
badge.

Load one with **Export → Save, load & contact sheet → Load**. It joins the Saved
cards shelf, so you can load several and tick the ones you want ganged onto a
single Sheet PDF.

| brightwater-dental | Standing Panel | dental |

Regenerate them after changing a composition — they are snapshots, not live
references, so a layout edit does not reach a card already saved.

The Google badge is your file. Its trademark position is unchanged and is
written up in `icons/README.md`; nothing here is a licence.
