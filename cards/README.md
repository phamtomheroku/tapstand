# cards/

Fifteen finished cards. Ten are credit cards (85.6 × 54 mm), one on each of the
landscape compositions. Five more are built on the three compositions that make
the review badge the subject rather than the signature — three at credit-card
size, two on the 102 × 152 mm counter card, where an upright trim gives the
portrait lockup the height it wants.

Every card wears the contactless hand mark and the Google review badge.

Load one with **Export → Save, load & contact sheet → Load**. It joins the Saved
cards shelf, so you can load several and tick the ones you want ganged onto a
single Sheet PDF.
